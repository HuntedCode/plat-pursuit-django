{% load static humanize %}{% comment %} Profile Card -- the share image for a hunter's whole career. Landscape only, 1200x630. The third sibling in the share-card family. Ground, scrims, Frame, identity strip, brand block and hairlines are the plat card's verbatim (pinned by tests/engine/test_profile_card.py the same way the recap card is pinned), so the three cards read as one product. What changes is the subject: the plat card records ONE completion; this records the HUNTER -- their platinum count, their trophy tallies, their badge collection, and their Pursuer standing. WHY IT LOOKS LIKE THIS - The accent is the platinum cyan (#27ebfe) throughout. The plat card splits cyan/amber by variant; a career has no variant, and cyan is the house colour the brand block already wears. - ZONES, same as the plat card: identity / the career / the spine. The middle zone leads with the platinum count as the single dominant statement (the "game title" slot) because it is the number trophy hunters actually introduce themselves with. The disciplines ring sits beside it: trophy record on the right, Pursuer identity on the left, one glance each. - The ring is the SAME precomputed geometry the Career hero renders (career_service supplies dash/offset for r=42 in a 120x120 viewBox); only the colours are inlined here, because this document has no stylesheet and no custom properties. - The spine band shows the badge collection the way the plat card shows one badge: real medallion art (subject only, no plate, no mask -- the silhouette IS the badge), newest first, with the held-of-catalog count beside it. The dominant discipline closes the band on the right. - NO comparisons, no rarity percentile of the hunter themselves. The card is a self-portrait, not a leaderboard row. INLINE STYLES, HEX ONLY -- Playwright renders via page.set_content() in an about:blank origin: no stylesheet, no custom properties. The --pp-* map is the same one documented at the top of plat_card.html; discipline/medallion/tier hexes come from completion_card_service's maps. Context: core/services/profile_card_service.get_card_data + api/shareable_views.build_profile_card_context. {% endcomment %}
