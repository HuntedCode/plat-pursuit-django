{% comment %} The Job Coverage card's board: FIVE SHELVES, one per discipline (owner's pick, 2026-10-08), in the radar's order and in each discipline's colour -- the same grouping the live board draws, because 25 jobs are really five groups of five and laying them out as one strip broke every group across a row. Each shelf is two covers across and three deep, and the sixth cell is the discipline's tally (4/5): the one number a hunter reading a shelf wants, in the space the 2x3 arrangement leaves free anyway. (It was three across and two deep first, at 64x85, and the art was too small to make out. Ten columns rather than fifteen is what gets the covers to the A-Z card's size.) GEOMETRY IS COMPUTED, in `share_card._shelf_geometry`, because the catalogue can change the board's shape: a deleted `Job` puts its square on a SIXTH shelf, and a staff edit to `Job.discipline` makes a shelf of SIX. Both overflowed the card when this was hardcoded. On the designed shape (five shelves of five) it comes out at the designed numbers: 78x104 covers, 162px shelves, ~75px between them with `space-between`. Expects: shelves and board (share_card.build_card_context), is_complete. {% endcomment %}
