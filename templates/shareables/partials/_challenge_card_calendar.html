{% comment %} The Plat Calendar card's board: the run page's YEAR OVERVIEW (owner's pick, 2026-10-08), twelve month rows by up to 31 day columns, so a hunter sharing it sees the year exactly as their run page draws it. ONE LENS. A filled day is a shovelware-free platinum earned on that date in any year -- the only board the Calendar has (the three-view model was collapsed on 2026-10-04). A filled day wears its MONTH's hue, the page's `--cal-c` table, ported to `share_card.MONTH_HUES`. NO NUMERALS IN THE SQUARES, unlike the page from `md:` up. The page can afford them because a reader is looking at the page; this travels to a Discord embed at ~37% scale, where a 12px numeral is 4px of noise. A strong fill reads at a glance at any size, and the ruler above gives the columns their dates. A STRUCK MONTH WEARS ITS HUE ON ITS LABEL AND TALLY, which the page deliberately does not ("the hue never touches text": `.pp-cal__ylabel` stays `--pp-text-dim`). The page can mark a struck month with its crest; the card has no crests, so the label is the only place to say it. The hues sit at oklch lightness 0.66 to 0.90 on a near-black ground, so the text stays readable. A RUN WITH NO DAY ROWS draws nothing below the ruler's place: `calendar_groups` returns no months for it (a board frame around nothing is the failure it guards against), and the ruler is skipped with them. GEOMETRY: 26px squares, 4px apart: 31 columns is 926px, beside a 52px month column and a 60px tally, and 12 rows plus the ruler is ~376px, which is what the header and the slim plaque leave. Expects: months and day_numbers (share_card._calendar_context). {% endcomment %}
