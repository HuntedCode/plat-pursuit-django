How your Career works

Your PlayStation history becomes a career. Three pieces to know:

1
25 jobs, five disciplines

Each job captures a different niche in gaming, from open worlds to the arcade and everything in between.

2
Contracts

Curated sets of games, each assigned jobs based on its genre and theme.

3
Pursuer

Complete contracts (finish the games, then claim the contract) to earn XP toward your jobs and your overall Pursuer level.
