    ` is a full-height spacer (total x --lb-row-h), so the page scrollbar spans the whole board and only the visible rows live in the DOM, placed absolutely by `PlatPursuit.virtualBoard`. No pager: pagination and a spacer are two answers to the same question, and this is the one that lets a reader move around a board freely. The FIRST window is server-rendered inside the spacer, so the board is there on arrival and on a no-JS read; every window after it comes from `rows_url`. The engine adopts these rows rather than re-fetching. WHY THIS IS ONE FILE. Three boards rendering their own shell is three sets of data attributes that must agree with one JS contract, and the failure is silent -- a board that omits `data-lb-page-size` does not break, it just fetches in the wrong granularity and shows gaps. Everything the client needs rides on the root as data, so the JS reads no constants of its own.
