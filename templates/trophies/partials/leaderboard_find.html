{% comment %} The hunter search, filling `leaderboard_jumpbar.html`'s slot between "you're #N" and the rank box. Shared by every board that has one. Type a name, pick a hunter, land on their rank. PREFIX-matched and scoped to the board being read, so a suggestion always carries a rank that exists on the list you are looking at -- the navbar already answers "find any hunter anywhere", and this answers the different question. Self-contained by design: the slot is included with `only`, so this reads nothing from the jump bar. `wireBoard` finds these three elements by their data attributes and wires `PlatPursuit.wireBoardSearch` against the board's own `?suggest=`. It reuses the shared `[data-search-wrap]` chrome (clear button + in-flight spinner) that the browse and collection pages use. Game detail has its own copy of this markup rather than including it: its field additionally previews the hunter at a typed RANK (`?at=`), which no other board offers, and it is mirrored into that page's minibar. {% endcomment %}
