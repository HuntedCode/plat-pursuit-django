Community Guidelines

A few ground rules keep PlatPursuit welcoming. By posting public content you agree to follow them.

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
  • Keep it family-friendly. No NSFW content, explicit language, or mature themes.
  • Mark spoilers. Warn before plot points, endings, or major reveals. Respect others' first playthrough.
  • Stay on topic. Keep it about the game, its trophies, and PlayStation gaming.
  • No spam or self-promotion. No ads, repetitive posts, or funneling people off-site.
  • Be helpful and constructive. Share tips and honest impressions; keep criticism respectful.
  • Follow platform rules. Obey PlayStation's Terms of Service and the law; no exploits or cheat methods.
  • Report, don't retaliate. Flag rule-breaking content instead of engaging with it.
  • Protect privacy. Don't share personal information about yourself or others.
