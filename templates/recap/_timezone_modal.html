{% comment %} First-run timezone confirmation. Why it exists: the timezone decides which month a trophy falls into. A trophy earned at 23:00 on the 31st belongs to the month the hunter experienced it in, and getting that wrong quietly mis-files their whole archive. `user_timezone` defaults to 'UTC' and is non-null, so it cannot tell us whether a UTC hunter is in London or has simply never been asked -- `timezone_confirmed_at` is what answers that. Why it is a CONFIRMATION and not a picker: the browser already knows the answer. Opening a list of eighty zones and asking someone to find themselves is a chore; showing them the zone they are almost certainly in and asking "right?" is one tap. The full picker is behind "Pick a different one" for the cases where the guess is wrong (a VPN, a traveller, a shared machine). Opens by itself only when `timezone_confirmed_at` is null AND this device has not dismissed it. Reopened any time from the header button. {% endcomment %}
