{% comment %} Timezone, as a quiet utility row. It decides which month a trophy falls into (a trophy earned at 23:00 on the 31st belongs to the month the hunter experienced it in, not the UTC one), so it has to be findable from the archive it governs. But it is a SETTING, and the previous treatment gave it the same weight as the recaps themselves: a full card with a bordered select, sitting directly under the page header and above the thing people came for. The `#recap-timezone-select` id and its `data-current` are the contract with _timezone_section_js.html, which fills the options and handles the change. {% endcomment %}

Changing it rebuilds your recaps. Manage in settings
