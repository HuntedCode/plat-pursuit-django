{% load static humanize %}{% comment %} Recap card -- the share image for a month. Landscape only, 1200x630. SIBLING OF THE PLAT CARD, NOT A COPY. Someone who has seen one should recognise where the other came from before they read a word, so everything that carries the identity is shared verbatim with `shareables/plat_card.html`: the ground, the two inner scrims, the Frame, the 40x46 padding, the identity strip and its brand block, the hairline rule at rgba(64,72,83,0.55), and the two-voice type. What differs is the SUBJECT, so what differs is the middle: a plat card is about one game, this is about a month, so the month leads and the evidence is a spread rather than a single cover. WHY IT LOOKS LIKE THIS - 1200x630 is the OG/Twitter/Discord embed ratio, and that is where these are seen -- about 450px wide in a Discord preview, so ~37% scale. Nothing here is sized below what survives that. The same constraint as the plat card, and it governs the TYPE SCALE, not the amount of information: the card is zoned (identity / the month / the proof) so density reads as rows. - THE MONTH IS THE HEADLINE. Not the trophy count. "March 2026" is what the card is of; the count is what it says. A card that leads with a number is a stat readout, and a stat readout is not something anybody posts. - The house accent, #27ebfe, and NOT a per-card variant. The plat card switches accent by variant (cyan platinum / amber 100%) because those are two states of one thing. A recap has no variants, so it takes the brand colour -- which is also the strongest "same place" signal the card has. - PLATINUMS ARE THE PROOF, shown as covers. A month's platinums are the part of it worth showing off, and cover art is the only thing on this card that is instantly readable at Discord scale. Eight slots, and the builder reports the overflow separately so "+N more" can actually fire -- it used to cap at three while the grid held eight, so the two counts could never differ. - THE ACTIVITY CALENDAR EARNS ITS SPACE. It is the one element that makes this read as a MONTH rather than a total: a figure says how much, the grid says how it happened -- a fortnight's binge and a steady thirty days can share a trophy count and are not the same month. It was dropped in the first pass of this rebuild and the card immediately felt barren, which is also what left the right of the card empty. Its ramp climbs in BOTH size and colour (`CALENDAR_RAMP`, a literal port of `.activity-level-*` in recap-deck.css) so it never depends on hue alone, and a day carrying a platinum takes a warm ring instead of more colour -- level 4 is "busy", the ring is "you closed something out", and those are different facts that must not collapse into one. - No community comparisons, same reasoning as the plat card: our population selects for completionists, so any "vs other hunters" figure reads systematically less impressive than it is. INLINE STYLES, HEX ONLY Playwright renders via page.set_content() in an about:blank origin: no stylesheet, no custom properties. So the --pp-* token VALUES are ported by hand. Keep this map in sync with input.css AND with the same map at the top of shareables/plat_card.html: --pp-bg-0 #05080c --pp-text #f0f6fd --pp-bg-1 #181d23 --pp-text-dim #9da5b1 --pp-bg-2 #232a31 --pp-text-mute #8a939f --pp-bg-3 #2f363f --pp-border #404853 --pp-primary #27ebfe Tier dot colours are TIER_DISPLAY from completion_card_service, so the trophy tiers read identically on both cards. TYPE: Bricolage Grotesque (--pp-font-display) for what you read first -- month, tallies, username, wordmark -- and Inter (--pp-font-body) for labels and meta. Both are embedded by the renderer from static/fonts/; a face that is not in that directory cannot render here at all, so keep to those two. Context: api/recap_views.RecapShareImageHTMLView._build_template_context. {% endcomment %}
