{% comment %} EMAIL BASE v2 -- the scaffold every rebuilt PlatPursuit email extends. WHY A v2 RATHER THAN AN EDIT: the legacy base (base_email.html) is div-based with no table scaffold, no MSO handling, no preheader, a non-bulletproof anchor CTA, and a #667eea purple that appears nowhere in the site's brand. Both bases coexist while the remaining children migrate one rebuild at a time; docs/guides/email-setup.md carries the migration table. When the last child moves, the legacy base and that table row die together. THE COLOUR SCHEME DECISION (settled, do not relitigate per-child): LIGHT content body with DARK brand bands top and bottom. - Gmail and Outlook apply their own forced transforms in dark mode, and they are worst on committed-dark email: a near-black body with near-white text is exactly the scheme partial inversion mangles into grey-on-grey. A light content body transforms predictably everywhere. - The brand still lands: the dark header and footer bands carry the logo, wordmark and the cyan accent, and small dark blocks survive inversion far better than dark body copy. - A committed-dark email also reads as promotional, which is wrong for the transactional genre these templates serve. We declare `color-scheme: light` so clients that honour the meta leave us alone, and tune a few tokens under prefers-color-scheme for Apple Mail (the one major client that honours it faithfully). THE PLAINTEXT RULE (load-bearing, pinned by tests/engine/test_auth_pages.py): there are no .txt templates -- the text/plain part of every email is the HTML with {% block extra_styles %}{% endblock %} {% comment %} The inbox preview line, then a run of zero-width joiners so the client does not pull the first body sentence in after it. {% endcomment %}
