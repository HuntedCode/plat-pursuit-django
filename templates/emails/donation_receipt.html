This is the only email in the family that is a RECORD as well as a thank-you: it is what a donor keeps, and what they quote back at us if a charge is ever queried. The detail table therefore carries the transaction id and the date, which the previous version did not print at all. `badge_picks_earned` forks the closing half and MUST be preserved: above zero it is the only place a donor is told they have picks waiting to spend, and the CTA changes with it. The date is labelled UTC because it is rendered in UTC: `TIME_ZONE` is UTC and the webhook that sends this activates no timezone, so a donor west of UTC paying in the evening gets a receipt dated the next day. Labelling it is cheaper than pretending, and a receipt with an unexplained off-by-one date is exactly the kind of thing that generates support mail. The amount keeps `floatformat:2` -- `amount` is a Decimal, and Decimal('25.5') renders as "25.5" without it, which reads as a truncated number on a receipt. `get_provider_display` comes from the model's own choices; the old `|title` filter rendered PayPal as "Paypal". {% endcomment %} {% block title %}Thank you for your donation!{% endblock %} {% block preheader %}Your donation to the {{ fundraiser.name }} is in.
