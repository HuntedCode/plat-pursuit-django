EVIDENCE-LED, NOT PROSE-LED --------------------------- An earlier version of this page told the story in four beats of paragraphs and read as a bombardment. The reference class here is Obsidian Catalyst, Blender's Development Fund, Home Assistant Cloud and the Wikipedia appeal: free products funded by the people who use them. What they share is that the page is mostly NOT text. It is a short statement plus evidence you can look at, and one human voice. So: the commissioned badge artwork does the persuading, the roadmap is a strip you scan rather than paragraphs you read, and the story is four sentences signed by a person. Everything longer belongs on `/support/roadmap/` or About, where someone has chosen to go deeper. Two rules carried over and still load-bearing: - THE PAST RENDERS IN NUMBERS, THE FUTURE RENDERS IN WORDS. The figures band and the shipped list carry counts; the roadmap strip carries no date, count or percentage. Pinned by test. - Every perk fills in BOTH columns, because premium is a dial and not a door. A row that could not fill "Everyone" would be a wall. Copy is Jeffrey's voice: earnest and ADDITIVE, never oppositional. Do not tighten it into ad copy.
