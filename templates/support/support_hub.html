Served by users.views.SupportStorefrontView, which also answers this page's checkout POST (the form carries no action, so handler and form share a URL). STRUCTURE --------- Stacked chrome CARDS, site convention, all the way down. An earlier version set prose directly on the page substrate, which nothing else here does and which read as foreign -- "free content" in this codebase means page-filling grids of items, never paragraphs. A discrete prose or stats section still gets a surface. The header is SPLIT: the statement on the left, the ask on the right in a decorated box with a `.pp-switch` for billing cycle. That puts the decision above the fold instead of below four sections of argument, and it means nothing further down has to work as hard. Everything after the header is evidence rather than prose: the commissioned artwork, a plat card, the transparency row, the supporter wall, a roadmap strip you scan, and one short signed note. The reference class is Obsidian Catalyst, Blender's Development Fund and the Wikipedia appeal -- free products funded by their users, where the page is mostly not text. THE LADDER IS PLACEHOLDERS RIGHT NOW. Buttons are inert until the twelve Stripe prices and twelve PayPal plans exist; the POST handler, success_url and webhooks underneath are untouched, so switching it on is wiring a button to a contract that already works. `tiers_are_placeholders` is forced False in live mode by the view, so production can never render a dead buy button. RULES THAT ARE PINNED BY TESTS - Every level gets every perk; only RECOGNITION escalates. Nothing is ever gated. - The perk table fills BOTH columns on every row -- a row that could not is a wall. - The past renders in numbers, the future renders in words: the roadmap strip carries no date, count or percentage. - Figures and the supporter wall are omitted whole when empty, never rendered as zeroes. Copy is Jeffrey's voice: earnest and ADDITIVE, never oppositional. Do not tighten it into ad copy. {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
