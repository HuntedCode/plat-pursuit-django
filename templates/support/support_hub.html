WHY THIS PAGE IS SHAPED LIKE A STORY AND NOT A PRICING TABLE ------------------------------------------------------------ What is being sold is a TRAJECTORY, not a feature set: nothing here is locked, so there is no ladder of capabilities to compare. That makes the roadmap the hero, told as an arc -- where we started, where we are, where we're going, and the ask -- with everything else in support of it. Two rules the arc holds to, both load-bearing: 1. THE PAST RENDERS IN NUMBERS, THE FUTURE RENDERS IN WORDS. Beats 1 and 2 carry dates and live figures; beat 3 carries no dates, no percentages and no counts. The credibility gradient is visible in the typography itself, and the page cannot accidentally promise a timeline. 2. The spine's markers are filled for past and present and hollow for the future, for the same reason. It is a CHRONOLOGY, not a progress bar -- deliberately not `.pp-horizon`, which the design system says must always bind to a real percentage and never be decorative. This is a persuasion surface, which is rare here; almost every other rebuilt page presents a hunter's own data. So it is built at MARKETING density rather than dashboard density: one idea per screen, real measure on the prose, and a type scale roughly double what a data page uses. It also skips the standard header card, following `badge_how_it_works.html`, which established a bespoke hero for a page whose job is teaching rather than reporting. Copy is Jeffrey's, lightly edited. Do not "tighten" it into ad copy: the voice is earnest and ADDITIVE, never oppositional, and the softness is the voice rather than a lack of polish. Three viewer states, three shapes: anonymous (sign-in CTA on the options), signed-in non-member (the real buttons), member (a thank-you where the options were). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}

How it started

It started with a Discord server.

Two friends with a love of trophy hunting and a desire to build a community around it. We wanted to turn our favorite hobby into something more: to take trophy hunting from disjointed grinding to a complete, coherent experience.

In January 2026, PlatPursuit.com went live so we could start building the tools we had been dreaming about. It has been an amazing road of learning (and plenty of mistakes!), and we are proud to finally present Platinum Pursuit 1.0.

Today

Where we are now.

Since January we have rebuilt very nearly the whole site, one page at a time: a Career surface to give your hunting a shape, badges that are genuinely worth collecting, milestones, titles, and an awful lot of quiet work underneath that you would never see. We also completely removed ads to ensure every hunter has the same, consistent experience no matter what.

Those two friends have turned into a whole team of trophy lovers dedicated to cultivating the greatest community on the internet and delivering on our goals. While our community team grows, our development team stays small (just me!) to keep a consistent vision across every page of the site.

{% comment %} From here the markers go hollow and the numbers stop. Nothing below this line carries a date, a count or a percentage. {% endcomment %}

Ahead

Where we're going.

Soon

More ways to play. Challenges are getting the revamp we have wanted to give them for a long time, and there is a lot more coming beside them: quests, streaks, a much bigger badge library, and things worth earning with somewhere to actually spend them.

After that

My Stats returns as a proper engine rather than a page full of numbers, and the things we set aside to get 1.0 out of the door come back rebuilt: game lists, notifications and profile showcases. We would rather bring those back properly than quickly.

Further out

PlatPursuit in your pocket, with even more ways to track everything you do across the vast gaming landscape, not just on PlayStation.

Truthfully, we have so many more ideas and dreams that we cannot fit them all on this roadmap!

We have so much more to do, and we need your help to do it.

PlatPursuit lives off the small community we have cultivated. We have no investors, we display no ads, and we have no dreams of corporate takeovers. We merely want to create cool tools to make your gaming experience better.

Becoming a premium subscriber helps us pay our server costs each month, funds our future projects and gives you more of what you love.
