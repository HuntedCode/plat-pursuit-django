Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

Platinum Pursuit ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website platpursuit.com.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and username.
  • PlayStation Network Data: When you link your PSN account, we collect your PSN username and publicly available trophy data through the PlayStation Network API.
  • User-Generated Content: Any game ratings, written quick takes, or roadmap contributions you submit to our platform.
  • Discord Linking: If you connect your Discord account, we store your Discord user ID so our bot can grant server roles that match your standing here. Unlinking is available any time through the bot.

Public Trophy Data

Trophy hunting is a public pursuit, and PlatPursuit tracks publicly available PSN trophy data for hunters who have not registered here, the same data anyone can see on PlayStation Network. Profiles for unregistered hunters hold only that public data: no email, no account, nothing you have not already made public through PSN. If you register and link that PSN account, it becomes yours.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Log Data: Your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited.
  • Cookies: We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session and preferences.
  • Email Delivery Records: We keep a log of the emails our systems send you (what was sent, when, and whether it was delivered) so we can troubleshoot delivery problems. Your address is scrubbed from these records if you delete your account.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Display your trophy progress and achievements
  • Enable community features such as ratings, quick takes, and roadmaps
  • Send you service-related communications
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

PlayStation Network API

We retrieve publicly available trophy and profile data from Sony's PlayStation Network. This data is used solely to provide trophy tracking features.

Stripe and PayPal

For memberships and fundraiser donations, we use Stripe and PayPal for payment processing. Your card and bank details never touch our servers. Their use of your personal information is governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy and the PayPal Privacy Statement. We keep our own records of your membership status, billing history, and donations (never your payment card details) so your perks work and your support is credited.

Cloudflare

We use Cloudflare to serve the site and Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate traffic measurement. It is cookieless and does not track you across sites.

Advertising

PlatPursuit does not serve advertising. We show no ads to anyone, on any page, whether or not you are signed in or subscribed. We set no advertising cookies, run no ad network code, and share no data with advertisers. The site is funded by our members and community supporters.

Cookies

We set only essential cookies: a session cookie (sessionid) that keeps you signed in and a security cookie (csrftoken) that protects forms. We set no analytics cookies and no advertising cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling these two will affect signing in.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account yourself at any time from your Settings page. Here is exactly what deletion does:

  • Your sign-in, email address, and personal account data are permanently removed.
  • Your PSN trophy history remains public, the same as any hunter who never registered, and your game ratings stay with it so community averages stay honest.
  • Your written quick takes are erased permanently. We offer you a download of them before you confirm.
  • Stripe and PayPal retain their own transaction records, as payment processors are required to.

Your Rights and Choices

You can:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Delete your account yourself from Settings, no request needed
  • Choose whether your name appears on the supporter wall (members, from the membership page)
  • Hide hidden or zero-progress games from your public profile (from Settings)

We send no marketing email. The only emails you will receive are about your account, your membership billing, or a fundraiser you took part in.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, reach us on Discord or by email at support@platpursuit.com.
