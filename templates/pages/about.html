About

Platinum Pursuit.

A trophy tracker built by trophy hunters, for trophy hunters. Sync your PlayStation trophies and your history becomes badges to earn, a Career to level, challenges to run and a recap of every month.

What you can do here

Track every trophy Every game, trophy list and platinum you have, with rarity and progress, kept in sync with PSN. Badges Over a hundred badge series with handcrafted art, earned by the games you platinum. Career and Jobs Twenty-five jobs across five disciplines. Finish the games a Contract covers, claim it, and your jobs level up. Challenges A-Z, Job Coverage and Plat Calendar runs, planned with the Challenge Optimizer. Game Lists A backlog, a top ten, the ones you mean to get back to. Keep them private or publish them. Leaderboards Badges, Rarity Score and Shovelware Free, plus a board on every badge series. Monthly Recap Every month of your pursuit, replayed: your trophies, your streaks, your rarest grabs and a share card.

Who we are

A small indie team of trophy hunters. No ads, ever: the site is funded by its members and supporters.
