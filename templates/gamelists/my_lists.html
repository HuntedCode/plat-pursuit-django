A truly disabled button takes no pointer events (so its title never renders as a tooltip) and leaves the tab order (so it can never be focused or announced) -- the at-cap explanation was unreachable by every route including the mouse. This stays focusable and announced, and the reason is rendered as visible text below rather than hidden in an attribute.
