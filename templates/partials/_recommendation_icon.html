{% comment %} One glyph per recommendation, in one file. 👍 green Do it -- send someone after this platinum ~ yellow Great game, rough platinum -- the qualified answer 👎 red Skip it -- don't Thumbs for the two ends because they are the universal signs for exactly this question, and a TILDE for the middle: "so-so" has no thumb, and the alternatives all pull the wrong way -- a half-thumb reads as a worse thumbs-up rather than a different answer, and a broken heart (what this first shipped as) says the game let you down when the middle option means the opposite, that the game was good and the platinum was not. Colour comes from `--rec-c` on the host, which every surface maps the same way (success / warning / error). The glyph carries no colour of its own, so an answer cannot end up green in one place and grey in another. Shared because the form, the community split on game detail, and anything that shows a verdict later must agree -- the same answer looking different in two places is the drift this whole feature keeps removing.
