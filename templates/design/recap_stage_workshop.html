Recap stage

Three answers to what the monthly recap actually is. The slides inside are the real partials, identical in all three, so the only thing being compared is the presentation around them. Click through each one: the motion is most of the argument.

{# ---------- A ---------- #}
A Ceremony The recap as an event

Full-bleed takeover on the same grammar as the claim ceremony: the page recedes, chrome goes, and you are somewhere else. Story controls people already know (segmented bar, tap left/right, auto-advance), and a colour world per beat so the whole surface changes character, not just the card. Transitions are directional with a spring settle, so the exit is choreographed as carefully as the entrance.

B Broadcast The recap as an artifact

The love-letter rule, cashed in: a console system readout your machine produced for you. Geometric ground, the Horizon as the floor, type doing the work, and beats that assemble into place rather than slide past. Slower and more prestigious than A; it feels like a document you were issued rather than a story you are being shown. Least familiar of the three, and the most ours.

C Spread The recap as a feature

Not a deck at all. One editorial column, beats revealing on scroll along a spine. Nothing to enter or exit, every beat is linkable, and it is the only direction that suits browsing back through old months casually rather than sitting through them. The trade: no pacing, no anticipation, and the quizzes lose their reason to exist.
