Build a Career

Every trophy you have ever earned feeds a Pursuer Level and 25 jobs across five disciplines. Contracts are the job board: accept one, finish its games, claim the XP. Your history alone will level you up the moment this sync lands.

Chase Badges

Badges are themed sets of games with hand-drawn medallion art. Platinum every game in a set to claim its medallion, and finish the whole ladder to turn it holographic. Some of yours may already be earned before you even start.

Your library, organized

Every game you have played becomes a browsable collection with progress, rarity, and community ratings. Rate My Games keeps the verdicts: which platinums were worth it, and how long they really took.

Months worth keeping

Every month closes with a Monthly Recap of your hunt, and every platinum earns a share card with your scores on it. Made to be posted, built from your real numbers.

Boards to climb

Global and country leaderboards, plus a board for every badge and every job. Once this sync finishes, you are on them.
