{{ challenge.total_slots }} days, one square for each. A day fills with a shovelware-free platinum earned on that date, in any year.

1
It fills itself

Your whole platinum history counts, and every new platinum fills its day when your profile syncs. There is nothing to pick.

2
Titles along the way

A title at {% for days in calendar_rungs %}{% if forloop.last %} and {% elif not forloop.first %}, {% endif %}{{ days }}{% endfor %} days. The last one finishes the run.

3
Open any day

See every game that filled it. One day can hold several platinums.

4
Your clock, your days

A platinum counts on the date you earned it in your own timezone. One earned on 29 February counts for 28 February.
