{% comment %}The toolbar both public challenge pages share: hunter search, the type switcher, and sort. ONE PARTIAL FOR TWO PAGES, taking `browse_url` and `form_id` from the host. The controls are identical; only the endpoint they submit to and the sorts on offer differ, and both of those are data. THE TYPE SWITCHER IS RADIO INPUTS INSIDE THE FORM, not a row of links, and that is the whole reason it is built this way -- `franchise_list.html` records it: switching preserves the active search and sort, native checked state and the browser Back button stay correct with no JS, and everything auto-applies through `browse-filters.js`. A switcher made of anchors would drop `?q=` and `?sort=` on every press. `data-live-search` + `data-page-search` are the shared filter controller's hooks; `hx-indicator` points at the grid so the spinner is the thing being replaced.{% endcomment %}
