Pursuer Avatar Lab

Three directions for an avatar drawn entirely in code. Every part is a shape with settings, so one part becomes many looks. Your rank sets the finish, your discipline sets the color, and badges, Contracts and milestones unlock parts. This same character becomes your Pursuer in the Frontier.

Where parts come from

Unlocks follow rules, so a new badge or Contract produces its part automatically. Nobody authors one unlock at a time.

Identity is always free

Face, skin, hair and eyes never lock. Unlocks are for gear, symbols and finish, the things you earn.

Saved as a recipe

An avatar is stored as a list of parts plus a recipe version. Redrawing a part later never changes anyone's saved look.

One character, two homes

The avatar you build on the site is your Pursuer in the Frontier, and gear you find there shows up here.

Readable at nav size
